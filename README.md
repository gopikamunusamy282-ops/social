Social Media Privacy and Digital Footprint

## 📌 Project Overview

Social media privacy and digital footprint are important aspects of online safety. Every activity performed on the internet, such as sharing photos, posting comments, liking posts, and visiting websites, may leave a digital footprint. This project explains how to protect personal information and maintain a safe online presence.

## 🎯 Objectives

- To understand social media privacy.
- To learn about digital footprints.
- To identify the risks of sharing personal information online.
- To understand how to protect personal data on social media.
- To promote safe and responsible internet usage.

## 🔐 What Is Social Media Privacy?

Social media privacy refers to protecting personal information shared on platforms such as Instagram, Facebook, WhatsApp, and other social networking sites. Privacy settings help users control who can view their posts, photos, and personal details.

## 🌐 What Is a Digital Footprint?

A digital footprint is the trail of information a person leaves behind while using the internet.

### Types of Digital Footprints

1. **Active Digital Footprint:** Information intentionally shared online, such as posts, comments, photos, and likes.
2. **Passive Digital Footprint:** Information collected during online activities, such as browsing data, cookies, and location data.

## ⚠️ Risks of Poor Online Privacy

- Identity theft and online scams.
- Misuse of personal photographs and information.
- Cyberbullying and unwanted contact.
- Loss of privacy.
- Damage to personal or professional reputation.

## 🛡️ Safety Measures

- Use strong and unique passwords.
- Enable two-factor authentication.
- Set social media accounts to private when appropriate.
- Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, addresses, and live location publicly.
- Check privacy settings regularly.
- Think before posting or sharing content.
- Avoid suspicious links and unknown requests.
- Review app permissions and remove access that is no longer needed.
